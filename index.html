How I work

Four habits from the field

  1. Start from the data

    Size the opportunity from the numbers before debating it.

  2. Align the top, then plan from the bottom

    The C-suite agrees on what to pursue; the people who deliver build the plan.

  3. Go to the front line

    Workshops, ride-alongs, RFP rooms. The best practices are usually already in the field.

  4. Track it to the P&L

    Control towers and ways of working so results hold after the program ends.
